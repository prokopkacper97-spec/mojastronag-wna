About

Clinical and
academic profile

I am a neurosurgery resident and PhD candidate at the Department of Neurosurgery, Medical University of Białystok. I combine clinical practice with research into optic nerve and orbital disorders and cerebrospinal fluid circulation.

My academic work focuses on mechanism-based surgical treatment of visual dysfunction, including optic nerve sheath fenestration and endoscopic optic nerve decompression. My experience includes clinical data analysis, ophthalmological outcome assessment, radiological interpretation, and evaluation of CSF hydrodynamics using lumbar infusion testing and ICM+ software.

Alongside my clinical and academic work, I develop NeuroProgress — a private learning system for neurosurgeons and residents that brings together structured planning, anatomy, a resource library, and measurable progress.
